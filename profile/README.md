# CodeJungle

A Polish software house building B2B systems, mobile apps and games since 2018. Our main focus right now is **[LiteTMS](https://litetms.eu)**, a cloud transport management system (TMS) for road carriers and freight forwarders in Europe.

## LiteTMS

LiteTMS keeps the information needed to plan, run and settle transport orders in one workspace.

- **Web app for the office:** orders and planning, employees, vehicles and trailers, contractors, documents and order finances.
- **LiteTMS Mobile** for iOS and Android, for drivers and office users: assigned orders, status updates, company chat and documents.
- **Optional modules** from the in-app Marketplace, such as CRM and 3D load planning.
